# Third-party material

The root MIT license applies only to the original RetryScope implementation,
scripts and original documentation. Source snapshots under evidence/source-snapshots
are unmodified copies of installed modules, for examination rather than execution
as a substitute for the packages. They remain under their upstream licenses.

The corresponding distribution metadata, copyright notices and license text are
in evidence/source-snapshots/licenses/. The pip-vendored directory additionally
contains the old HTTP implementation license notices and their provenance.
Exact source bytes and hashes are recorded in evidence/environment.json.
Requests and botocore use Apache-2.0; urllib3, pip, smart_open and retrying retain
the licenses provided by their distributions; consult the included text rather
than relying on this summary. Hugging Face Hub retains its Apache-2.0 license.
No research-paper PDF, private conversation, account credential, or unrelated
manuscript is included. The manuscript bundle's unchanged IEEEtran class/style
files retain their own headers and license conditions.

The artifact additionally retains unmodified Pooch, fsspec, aiohttp and
HTTPX modules under evidence/extension-source/. Their installed METADATA and
license/author notices are in that directory's licenses/ subtree. The exact
snapshot and notice hashes are in evidence/extension-environment.json. These
files are evidence, not a replacement runtime. No font files are distributed.
